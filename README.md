# Skin Clinic Brilliance

We want to draft a elegant and engaging website for Tha Skin Clinic Mangalore https://www.theskinclinic.org.in/. They have a existing website but they need significant change in the design - more.classy and provides confidence to patients about their brand. Our competition has designed a site which we need to.improve and display better than this (https://theskinclinic-org-in-web.vercel.app/). We also need order their Formula V products and need a ecommerce order management feature. Also we need access to patient portal (which we will build later) for now just provision the patient portal login button. Share more knowledge and make it interactive

 Check their Instagram and take more data from Skin Clinic Instagram.
